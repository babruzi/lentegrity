The Legend of Lentegrity

It all started at a charity golf tournament, when Bob Lentini's foursome had the audacity to turn in a scorecard of 25 under par. Jaws dropped. Eyebrows went up. Some people still want to see that scorecard.

The questions followed Bob to Thursday Night Hockey. After the game, with the beers out in the parking lot, the guys called out his integrity, and a new word was born: Lentegrity.

Len·teg·ri·ty (n.) — the kind of integrity that turns in a -25 with a straight face and defends it in a parking lot.
